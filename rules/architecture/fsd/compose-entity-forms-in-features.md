---
name: compose-entity-forms-in-features
description: Assign ownership of entity forms across FSD layers.
---

The entity owns the form definitions for its actions, including fields, validation, and initial values. The feature owns the user action and composes the participating entities through their public APIs. The page places the ready-to-use feature and owns screen-specific layout, navigation, and completion behavior. These boundaries also apply to forms used on a single screen.
