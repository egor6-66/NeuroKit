---
name: group-by-responsibility
description: Organize related files by topic or responsibility.
---

Before adding a file, identify the feature, policy, or subject it belongs to.
Keep related files together in a directory named for their shared responsibility.
When the project structure or an agreed plan already identifies distinct groups, place new files in the appropriate group from the start.
If a new file forms a clear group with existing files, bring those files together and update their imports, configuration paths, and documentation references.
